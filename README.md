# Maven Toys Sales Dashboard

## Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing the sales performance of Maven Toys, a fictitious chain of toy stores in Mexico, by product category, store location, and month.

## Objectives

- Track key sales metrics: quantity, cost, revenue, and profit.
- Compare performance across product categories and store locations.
- Present monthly trends with interactive filters.

## My Role

Case study | 2026

- Cleaned and combined sales, product, and store data.
- Calculated revenue, cost, and profit by month, product category, and store location.
- Designed the dashboard with KPI cards, charts, and Month and Location slicers.
- Identified top-performing categories and locations.

## Key Insights

- Total revenue is 1,464,119 USD and total profit is 402,842 USD (27.5% margin) from 109,993 units sold.
- Downtown generates the most profit: 239,523 USD, or 59.5% of the total.
- Toys is the top category by revenue (about 391 thousand USD) and by units sold (28,309).
